Great Readings

This curated bibliography brings together field-defining monographs, seminal theoretical essays, landmark empirical studies, and transformative manifestos in Museum Studies. These readings challenge institutional dogmas, investigate how museums construct knowledge and power, and explore emerging paradigms in decolonization, visitor experience, and civic activism.
