# Great Courses

This curated collection gathers premier university lecture series, museum-produced MOOCs, and specialized professional training in **Museum Studies**. These courses go beyond introductory surveys to offer structured audiovisual deep dives into curatorial practice, collections care, visitor learning theory, digital transformation, and heritage protection.

- [Museum History, Theory & Institutional Governance](#museum-history-theory-institutional-governance)
- [Curatorial Practice, Interpretation & Material Culture](#curatorial-practice-interpretation-material-culture)
- [Museum Education, Inquiry & Community Engagement](#museum-education-inquiry-community-engagement)
- [Collections Care, Conservation & Storage Management](#collections-care-conservation-storage-management)
- [Cultural Property Law, Art Crime & Repatriation](#cultural-property-law-art-crime-repatriation)
- [Digital Heritage & Collections Technology](#digital-heritage-collections-technology)
