Great Courses

This curated collection gathers premier university lecture series, museum-produced MOOCs, and specialized professional training in Museum Studies. These courses go beyond introductory surveys to offer structured audiovisual deep dives into curatorial practice, collections care, visitor learning theory, digital transformation, and heritage protection.

  • Museum History, Theory & Institutional Governance
  • Curatorial Practice, Interpretation & Material Culture
  • Museum Education, Inquiry & Community Engagement
  • Collections Care, Conservation & Storage Management
  • Cultural Property Law, Art Crime & Repatriation
  • Digital Heritage & Collections Technology

Museum History, Theory & Institutional Governance

  • Arts and Heritage Management (Coursera / Università Bocconi)

Curatorial Practice, Interpretation & Material Culture

Museum Education, Inquiry & Community Engagement

  • Using Museums to Develop Learning (FutureLearn / University of Glasgow)
  • Interdisciplinary Teaching with Museum Objects (edX / Smithsonian Institution)
