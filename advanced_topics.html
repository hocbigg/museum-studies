Advanced Topics

  • Critical Curatorial Studies & Contemporary Exhibition Theory - Investigates the intellectual histories of display, the spatial politics of gallery interiors, born-digital and time-based curation, and radical institutional interventions.
  • Digital Museology, Computational Heritage & Linked Data - Explores formal semantic web ontologies, 3D metric survey and volumetric capture, multimodal immersive environments, and artificial intelligence in collections stewardship.

Critical Curatorial Studies & Contemporary Exhibition Theory

This specialization examines the theoretical lineages of curating, the ideological mechanics of architectural display, methodologies for exhibiting dynamic media, and activist practices that challenge traditional institutional authority.

Exhibition Histories and Curatorial Discourse: The Culture of Curating and the Curating of Culture(s) (Paul O'Neill / MIT Press) - Traces the historical transformation of curating from collections custodianship to an autonomous, discourse-generating creative medium.

Spatial Politics and Gallery Scenography: Spaces of Experience: Art Gallery Interiors from 1800 to 2000 (Charlotte Klonk / Yale University Press) - Examines how interior gallery architecture, lighting strategies, and wall installations condition the spectator's cognitive and bodily experience.

Curating Time-Based and Networked Media: Rethinking Curating: Art after New Media (Beryl Graham and Sarah Cook / MIT Press) - Provides curatorial strategies and conceptual models for staging process-oriented, participatory, and software-driven digital art.

Radical Museology and Institutional Activism: Radical Museology: or, What's 'Contemporary' in Museums of Contemporary Art? (Claire Bishop / Walther König) - Advocates for politicized, anachronic collection displays that use historical works to critically confront present socio-political crises.

Digital Museology, Computational Heritage & Linked Data

This specialization covers the computational architectures, semantic standards, 3D recording methods, and algorithmic workflows transforming collection access and digital heritage preservation.

Semantic Web, Ontologies and Linked Open Data: CIDOC CRM Video Tutorial Series (ICS-FORTH / ICOM CIDOC) - A structured video training series teaching formal semantic modeling and mapping cultural data to the ISO 21127 standard.

3D Digitization and Metric Survey: Photogrammetric Applications for Cultural Heritage: Guidance for Good Practice (Historic England) - A technical field manual covering high-resolution image capture, structure-from-motion algorithms, and metric surface reconstruction.

Multimodal Scenography and Spatial Computing: Virtuality and the Art of Exhibition: Curatorial Design for the Multimedial Museum (Vince Dziekan / Intellect Books) - Explores how spatial computing, virtual extensions, and digital mediation transform physical museum galleries into multimodal environments.

Artificial Intelligence and Algorithmic Collections Stewardship: Artificial Intelligence and Cultural Heritage Hub (Europeana Pro) - Provides technical documentation, policy frameworks, and case studies on machine learning, computer vision, and automated metadata enhancement in cultural institutions.

Decolonial Praxis, Indigenous Curation & Community Museologies

This specialization focuses on decentering colonial institutional hierarchies through Indigenous methodologies, statutory repatriation workflows, radical co-curation, and the presentation of difficult histories.

Indigenous Methodologies and Decolonial Curation: Decolonizing Museums: Representing Native America in National and Tribal Museums (Amy Lonetree / University of North Carolina Press) - Details how Indigenous communities and critical curators challenge colonial narratives and address historical trauma inside major institutions.

Statutory Repatriation Protocols and Tribal Consultation: National NAGPRA Program Video Training Series (National Park Service) - Practical instructional videos covering regulatory definitions, collections summaries, inventories, consultation protocols, and federal compliance.

Community Co-Curation and Participatory Practice: The Participatory Museum (Nina Simon) - An open-access guide detailing practical frameworks for inviting visitor co-creation and sharing curatorial authority with communities.

Exhibiting Contested Heritage and Traumatic Memory: Difficult Heritage: Negotiating the Nazi Past in Nuremberg and Beyond (Sharon Macdonald / Routledge) - Investigates theoretical and practical frameworks for preserving, interpreting, and displaying sites and objects tied to traumatic national histories.

Conservation Science, Technical Art History & Heritage Emergency Management

This specialization investigates the chemical and structural diagnostic methods of technical art history, the preservation of variable digital media, and risk-management triage during armed conflict and climate disasters.

Technical Art History and Material Characterization: Conservation of Easel Paintings (Joyce Hill Stoner and Rebecca Rushfield, eds. / Routledge) - A comprehensive technical volume covering analytical spectroscopy, pigment chemistry, layer structure analysis, and material degradation.

Time-Based Media Conservation and Digital Art Preservation: Conservation of Time-Based Media Art (Deena Engel and Joanna Phillips, eds. / Routledge) - Addresses hardware obsolescence, source code analysis, software emulation, and artist intent documentation for vulnerable digital artworks.

Disaster Risk Management and Crisis Stabilization: First Aid to Cultural Heritage in Times of Crisis: Handbook and Toolkit (ICCROM / Aparna Tandon) - An actionable field toolkit detailing protocols for securing, documenting, and stabilizing damaged cultural collections during active emergencies.

Disaster Risk Management and Crisis Stabilization: Disaster Risk Management for Cultural Heritage Conference Series (Leibniz Research Network Preservation / Smithsonian / ICOM-CC) - Recorded international conference lectures presenting multi-hazard risk assessment models, climate adaptation strategies, and institutional salvage operations.

Restitution Ethics, Art Crime & Fiduciary Museum Policy

This specialization addresses the forensic tracking of looted antiquities networks, post-colonial bilateral restitution agreements, deaccessioning case law, and the critical ethics of institutional philanthropy.

Illicit Antiquities Trafficking and Transnational Art Crime: Antiquities Trafficking and Art Crime (University of Glasgow / FutureLearn / Trafficking Culture) - An audit-mode online course analyzing looting supply chains, grey market antiquities circulation, and international cultural property enforcement.

Post-Colonial Restitution and Transnational Heritage Ethics: The Restitution of African Cultural Heritage: Toward a New Relational Ethics (Felwine Sarr and Bénédicte Savoy / Ministère de la Culture) - A foundational open-access policy document outlining the moral, legal, and operational frameworks for state-level cultural restitution.

Post-Colonial Restitution and Transnational Heritage Ethics: The Brutish Museums: The Benin Bronzes, Colonial Violence and Cultural Restitution (Dan Hicks / Pluto Press) - Analyzes the military looting of the Benin Bronzes and demonstrates how ethnographic museums functioned as weapons of colonial subjugation.

Deaccessioning Jurisprudence and Collections Stewardship: Deaccessioning and Its Discontents: A Critical History (Martin Gammon / MIT Press) - The definitive historical and legal study examining museum collection disposals, fiduciary controversies, and public trust law.

Critical Museum Ethics, Philanthropic Governance and Labor: The Routledge Companion to Museum Ethics: Redefining Ethics for the Twenty-First-Century Museum (Janet Marstine, ed. / Routledge) - Explores systemic ethical challenges including corporate sponsorship conflicts of interest, trustee accountability, labor activism, and institutional transparency.
